Standardized effect size, (mean1 − mean2) / pooled SD.

Two-sample comparison, equal group sizes, normal-approximation to the noncentral t-distribution (Cohen's method). Adequate for typical metabolomics/proteomics-style continuous outcomes. Not appropriate for count-based omics data (RNA-seq, metagenomics) — those modes are planned separately and will use negative-binomial / overdispersed-count models rather than this t-test approximation.
