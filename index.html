MSB

Multi-Omics, Systems Biology & Bioinformatics

We apply multi-omics data integration and systems biology approaches to animal and veterinary science, combining genomics, transcriptomics, epigenomics, metagenomics, and metabolomics to understand health, disease resistance, and production traits in livestock and companion animals.

Principal Investigator & Members

  • Haja N Kadarmideen — Principal Investigator
  • Udayaraja GK — Multi-Omics Bioinformatician
  • Pratik Pandurang Pathade — PhD Student

Research Areas

Genomics

Variant discovery and genomic selection in livestock and companion animal populations.

Transcriptomics

RNA-seq profiling of gene expression across health, disease, and production phenotypes.

Epigenomics

DNA methylation and chromatin-based regulation of animal traits.

Metagenomics

Microbiome composition and function in gut, rumen, and reproductive tract health.

Metabolomics

Metabolite profiling linked to physiology, nutrition, and disease biomarkers.

Phenomics

High-throughput phenotyping connecting molecular data to observable animal traits.

Tools

Multi-Omics Power Analysis available

Sample size and power calculations for two-group, RNA-seq, and metagenomics study designs.
