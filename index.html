Research Areas

Genomics

Variant discovery and genomic selection in livestock and companion animal populations.

Transcriptomics

RNA-seq profiling of gene expression across health, disease, and production phenotypes.

Epigenomics

DNA methylation and chromatin-based regulation of animal traits.

Metagenomics

Microbiome composition and function in gut, rumen, and reproductive tract health.

Metabolomics

Metabolite profiling linked to physiology, nutrition, and disease biomarkers.
